Target Dataset Exploration: SQL & Python

Project Overview -
This project focuses on analyzing an online retail dataset to understand customer behavior, sales trends, product performance, and seller contribution.
By using SQL for querying and Python for analysis and visualization, the project converts raw data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

Business Objectives -
Analyze sales performance over time ,
Understand customer purchasing and retention behavior ,
Identify top-performing product categories and sellers ,
Measure payment trends and revenue contribution ,
Generate actionable insights for business growth

Dataset Information -
The analysis is based on 7 CSV files:
customers.csv – Customer demographic details ;
sellers.csv – Seller information ; 
orders.csv – Order history and timestamps ;
order_items.csv – Product-level order details ;
payments.csv – Payment type and value ;
products.csv – Product attributes and categories ;
geolocation.csv – Customer and seller location data

Tools & Technologies -
SQL (MySQL Workbench) – Data querying & aggregation  ;
Python (Pandas, Matplotlib) – Data analysis & visualization  ;
Google Colab – Interactive analysis and charts 

Key Insights -
Health & Beauty and Watches categories generate the highest revenue ,
Customer retention rate is around 30–35%, indicating scope for loyalty programs ,
Nearly 49% of orders are paid using installment options ,
Product price has very weak correlation (-0.03) with purchase frequency ,
A small group of sellers contributes significantly to total revenue

Business Impact -
Helps identify high-value customers and sellers ,
Supports better inventory and category planning ,
Provides insights for pricing and payment strategy optimization ,
Useful for designing customer retention initiatives

Conclusion -
This project demonstrates how SQL and Python together can be used to analyze real-world e-commerce data and extract meaningful insights.
The findings can help businesses improve sales strategy, customer engagement, and revenue planning.
